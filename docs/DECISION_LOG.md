# Research Decision Log

Record only decisions that future-you or Codex could otherwise reopen unnecessarily.

## Template

### D-YYYYMMDD-01 — Title

**Decision:**  
What was decided.

**Reason:**  
Why this choice was made.

**Evidence considered:**  
Files, experiments, reviewer concern, or page constraint.

**Rejected alternatives:**  
What was considered but not chosen.

**Revisit only if:**  
A concrete condition that would justify reopening the decision.
